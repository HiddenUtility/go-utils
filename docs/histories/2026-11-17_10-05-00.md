## Technical Decisions

- **Pure Go SQLite Driver**: Selected `modernc.org/sqlite` to ensure the library can be used in environments without CGO support.
- **Thread Safety**: Employed `sync.Mutex` within `SqlLogger` to protect the in-memory buffer and coordinate database operations, ensuring safe concurrent access.
- **Buffer-DB Synchronization**: Designed `GetRecs...` methods to merge in-memory buffered logs with database records, providing a real-time, consistent view of the logs.
- **Resource Management**: Implemented `Reduce` to balance the in-memory buffer and database records to strictly adhere to the `maxRecNum` constraint.
